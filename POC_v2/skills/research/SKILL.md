# Research

Understand the task and the relevant parts of the workspace. Inspect existing
code, tests, documentation, and conventions. Do not modify the implementation.

Write a concise `research.md` in the run's artifact directory containing:

- relevant files and current behavior;
- constraints, assumptions, and material risks;
- unanswered questions only when they prevent a safe plan.

Choose `complete` when the next stage has enough grounded context. Choose
`blocked` when essential evidence or access is unavailable.
